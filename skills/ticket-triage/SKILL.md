# Ticket triage playbook

For every ticket:

- **Priority**: P0 (now, wake people up), P1 (this sprint), P2 (next), P3 ("lol no", politely).
- **Owner**: a person or service team. "The team" is not an owner.
- **Missing info?** Ask the reporter for repro steps and the full error message.
- **Duplicate?** Close it with a kind link to the original.

Hand big batches to the triager crew member, then double-check the P0s yourself.
